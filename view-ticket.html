📜 Guidelines for QCBELC Members

As part of our fundraising efforts, members are encouraged to support the Digital Roaring Raffle and are asked to purchase a minimum of twenty (20) raffle tickets each, choosing either option below.

Option 1: Keep Your Tickets

Keep all 20 tickets as your personal donation and raffle entries — giving you twenty chances to win.

Option 2: Sell Your Tickets

Sell your assigned tickets to friends, family, or other interested buyers. To keep records accurate, please submit the new ticket holder's name and the matching raffle ticket number(s) to the raffle team.
