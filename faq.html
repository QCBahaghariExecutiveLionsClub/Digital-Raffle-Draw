1. Getting Started

It's a digital ticketing tool for the raffle. A member or volunteer collects payment from a donor/supporter, then generates a digital ticket for them. Each ticket gets its own unique QR code that the donor can scan anytime to view or share it.

Only QCBELC Lion's Club members, volunteers, and assistants who have been given the access PIN. The Generate Ticket page is locked with this PIN.

No. Everything works right inside your phone or computer's web browser (like Chrome or Safari). There is nothing to download.

2. Selling & Generating Tickets

No, there is no total limit at all. You may notice each submission allows a maximum of 10 tickets — that is only there to prevent a mistake (like an accidental typo). It does not limit how many tickets you can sell in total.

Simply submit it in smaller batches. For example, 100 tickets could be entered as ten separate submissions of 10 tickets each. Each submission only takes a few seconds.

💡 Tip: You can generate as many batches as needed, one right after another, for the same donor.

Yes. There is no "closing" or reset step. You can keep generating tickets for as long as the raffle is open — just keep using the same page and PIN.

One receipt photo is saved and linked to the whole batch — you do not need to upload it again for each ticket, since all tickets in that batch share the same proof of payment.

3. Ticket IDs & Safety

No. Every new Ticket ID and QR code is automatically checked against every ticket that already exists before it is saved. If a duplicate is ever detected, the system quietly creates a fresh one instead — so a duplicate can never be saved.

For a raffle of this size (hundreds to a few thousand tickets), there is effectively no limit — the ID system supports millions of unique combinations.

4. Sharing Tickets

They scan the QR code printed or shown on their ticket, or tap the "Open Full Digital Ticket" link that appears right after it's generated. This opens their ticket on their own phone.

Yes. Every generated ticket has simple share buttons for Facebook, X, and Email, plus a "Copy Link" option. There is also a "Share All" button to share every ticket in one batch together in a single message.

Yes. When viewing any one of their tickets, a big "View My Other Tickets" button appears if that ticket was part of a multi-ticket purchase — tapping it shows the rest of their tickets and whether each one is Verified.

Older tickets still work perfectly for viewing, verifying, and transferring ownership. They just won't show the "View My Other Tickets" button, since that connection wasn't saved into their QR code at the time they were made.

5. Leaving the Ticket Screen

This is a safety check so that a ticket is not accidentally lost before it's been saved or shared with the donor. If you try to close the tab, refresh, or tap a navigation link while tickets are showing, a small pop-up will ask "Have you saved or shared this ticket?" before letting you continue.

No. It only appears after tickets have actually been generated and are showing on screen — never while you're still typing in the form.

🔄

6. Giving or Reselling a Ticket

The new owner can update this themselves, right from the ticket page, by entering their name and a 4-character verification code. This code is simply the last 4 digits of the original transaction/reference number — known only to the seller and the original buyer, which keeps a stranger from changing ownership just by seeing a QR code.

No. The original donor's name always stays on record for our audit trail — only the "current holder" shown on the ticket updates.

7. Admin Panel

It gives the raffle organizer a complete, searchable list of every ticket ever generated, along with:

  • Total tickets generated, how many are Verified, how many are Pending, and total value raised
  • A search box to instantly find any ticket by buyer name, seller name, ticket ID, or transaction number
  • Filter tabs for "All", "✅ Verified", or "⏳ Pending" tickets
  • A "Reprint / View" button and QR code for every ticket, in case a donor needs it shown again
  • A "Mark Verified" button for any ticket still pending review

Yes, this has been fixed. The issue happened because some phone numbers and reference numbers were being stored as plain numbers instead of text, which quietly broke the search. The system now always treats these as text, so searching works reliably no matter what the buyer's phone or reference number looks like.

You'll now see a clear message on screen saying the tickets could not be loaded, instead of silently showing fake sample data. If you see this, check your internet connection and try refreshing the page.

At the scale of a typical Lion's Club raffle (hundreds to a few thousand tickets), it stays fast. It may feel slightly slower only if ticket counts grow into the tens of thousands — not something to worry about for a normal event.

🆘

8. If Something Goes Wrong

First, make sure the QR code was scanned directly (not a screenshot of a screenshot, which can be blurry). If it still doesn't work, contact the raffle organizer — the Admin Panel can look up any ticket directly by name or ID.

Please reach out to your raffle committee lead or the volunteer coordinator who gave you your access PIN.
